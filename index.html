Privacy Policy

Effective Date:

Thank you for choosing to use our QR Code & Barcode Scanner application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. Our App does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything you scan or generate using the App stays strictly on your device.

2. App Permissions

To provide our core functionality, the App requires certain permissions on your device. We use these permissions solely for the operation of the App:

  • Camera Permission: The App requires access to your device's camera to scan QR codes and barcodes in real-time. The camera feed is processed entirely on your device, and no video or image data is ever transmitted to our servers or any third parties.
  • Storage / Photo Gallery (Optional): If you choose to scan a QR code from an image saved on your device, the App may request access to your photo library. This access is strictly limited to the image you select for scanning.

3. Data Storage

The App features a "History" tab that allows you to view previously scanned and generated QR codes. This history data is saved locally on your device's internal storage. We do not have access to this data. You can delete your history directly within the App or by clearing the App's data in your device settings.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking, or ad-serving frameworks that collect your personal information. We do not share your data with advertisers or marketing companies.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
